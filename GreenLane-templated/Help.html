About Green Lane

Green Lane connects ambulance drivers and Traffic Police in real time. When a driver starts an emergency, nearby Traffic Police instantly see the ambulance's live location, distance and estimated arrival time — so the junction can be cleared before the ambulance even gets there.

Features

Live Emergency Alerts

Start an emergency as a driver and every nearby Traffic Police unit is notified instantly, with live distance and ETA.

Live Map Tracking

See the ambulance and Traffic Police units move on the map in real time, with route, direction and estimated speed.

Junction Status

Traffic Police can mark their junction as Ready, Traffic Managed, or Cleared — the driver sees this update immediately.

Notifications & Alerts

An audible alert and an on-screen banner play automatically when an ambulance is close to your junction.

How to Use

Sign up or log in as a Driver

Create an account from the Sign Up page and choose "I am a Driver".

Open the Live Map

From your dashboard, click "Open Full Map" or "Live Map" in the sidebar.

Allow location access

Your browser will ask for GPS permission - allow it so Traffic Police can see your live position.

Start Emergency

Tap "Start Emergency" the moment you're on a call. Nearby Traffic Police are alerted instantly.

Stop Emergency when done

Tap "Stop Emergency" once the ambulance has passed the junction, to clear the alert for everyone.

Sign up or log in as Traffic Police

Create an account from the Sign Up page and choose "I am a Police Officer".

Name your junction

On the Live Map page, type your junction's name (e.g. "MG Road Signal") and save it.

Allow location access

This lets the app calculate live distance and ETA from any active ambulance to your junction.

Tap the Mute Alert Sound button once

Browsers block alert sounds until you interact with the page - tapping this button (even just to check it) enables the emergency buzzer for your session.

Respond to alerts

When an ambulance is close, you'll see a banner and hear an alert. Update your junction status as you manage traffic.
